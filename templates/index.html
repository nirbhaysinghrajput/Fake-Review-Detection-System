About this system

This is a college project designed to evaluate product and service reviews for authenticity.

How it works:

  1. Text Preprocessing: Lowercasing, punctuation and URL removal, stopword filtering with sentiment preservation, and word normalization.
  2. Deep Learning Model: A Bidirectional LSTM neural network trained on balanced genuine and deceptive reviews.
  3. Pattern Heuristics: Inspects writing traits such as excessive capitalization, urgent promotional language, and repetitive terms.

Note: Predictions are probabilistic estimates intended for educational and analytical demonstration.
